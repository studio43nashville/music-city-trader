What the First Trading Cycle Taught Me

November 3–6, 2026

The first cycle started with three winning days, then ended when I took too many aggressive scalps at the New York open. I was up about $700 before losing about $1,000 in one scalp session and breaching the allowed-loss threshold.

The main lesson:

Do not scalp during the open. Wait for calmer levels, cleaner pullbacks, and a setup that gives the trade room to work.

Fees and speed worked against me

Entering too quickly and scalping too often allowed fees to eat into my liquidity. The faster I traded, the easier it became to interfere with the drawdown threshold and turn a manageable day into a blown evaluation.

The rules I’m carrying forward

  • Take no more than two trades per day when scalping.
  • Risk $100 per entry with 4 micro MNQ.
  • Cap the full-day loss at $200.
  • Use a tight pullback and take-profit plan.
  • Remember that a $100 winning day is still a win when the rules were followed.

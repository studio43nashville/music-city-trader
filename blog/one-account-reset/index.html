Why I’m Resetting to One Account

November 10, 2026

I’m taking a pause from trading until Monday, November 16. I traded across five accounts and did not follow my rules. Instead of continuing to reset every account, I’m resetting one account and using it to practice live while the other accounts remain untouched until they reset at the end of the month.

The new operating rule:

If I follow my rules and still lose, I can reset one account for continued practice. If I ignore my rules, I do not keep resetting all of my accounts.

The $200 daily loss limit

My daily loss limit is now $200. I can take one trade risking $200, or two smaller scalping trades risking $100 each. Once I lose $200, I stop for the day.

Why the reset matters

This is about slowing down and breaking the dopamine-driven cycle of impulsive entries. I need to prove that I can take one planned trade, manage the risk, and stop when the plan says to stop. Scaling back to one account gives me a clearer test of discipline.

The path forward

I’ll begin with one account on Monday, November 16. The longer-term plan can return to five followed accounts after the account reset in December, but only after I demonstrate that I can follow the rules consistently.

This is a personal trading journal and discipline plan, not financial advice or a promise of profit.

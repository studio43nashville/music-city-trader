Short overview retained for context; detailed entries have been cleared from the active journal.

November 3–6, 2026 overview. I had three winning days, then blew up on day four after taking crazy scalps at the New York open. I was up about $700, then lost about $1,000 in one scalp session. The scalp fees ate into my net liquidity and caused me to breach the limit. The key lesson is: do not scalp during the open. Stay away and wait for calmer-moving levels instead of trading a bunch of bouncing candles. I was doing well while testing ATM strategies and learned that entering too fast and scalping too often lets fees eat into the results and lowers the liquidity threshold I can reach. When I get too fast, I can easily blow up the evaluation; when I follow the rules, I do okay. Even while scalping, the plan is now to take only two trades per day, risk $100 per entry with 4 micro MNQ, and cap the full-day loss at $200. Each scalp needs a tight pullback and take-profit so I do not interfere with intraday drawdown. A $100 winning day is still a WIN—small wins are acceptable when I am following the plan. The previous evaluation period ended after the allowed-loss threshold was breached, and the next run will follow these rules across five accounts.

NOV 19

Profitable, but nighttime trading was reckless

The dashboard recorded +$54. I traded recklessly last night before bed and took a ton of trades. This morning was a much better session, but nighttime trading is not reliable for my strategy and was a waste of time. The Take Profit Trader dashboard shows a $25,594.50 balance and $594.50 net profit toward the $1,500 target, with $905.50 remaining. Going forward, I need to stop trading at night and focus on one definitive session so I do not rely on luck or put the profit back.

NOV 18

A green day, but the rules were broken

I traded from 7:15 to 8:50 AM and took more than 20 trades, including trades through the volatile open, which I should avoid. I was up and down repeatedly—never more than about $400 down—but finished the day up $127. Total cash was $25,862 and the liquidity level was $25,622; all those entries took a toll on liquidity. I came in feeling good because I was already way up, and that confidence turned dangerous. Fighting to finish green every day is not the same as being patient, and I do not think that combination is sustainable.

I may need a short break. If I trade tomorrow, I need to return to the full three-trade rule: no more than three trades, and after two losses in a row I am done for the day. For the next five trading sessions, the priority is practicing that stop-after-two-losses discipline—not forcing a green result.

NOV 17

Patient pre-open breakout

Traded at 7:30 AM during the pre-open. The market was boxed in, so I waited for clear levels to break before entering. I mixed snipes and longer positions, stayed patient, and followed the updated rules without taking two losses in a row. I was up about $250, then went back in during the afternoon and brought the day to $545. That extra session was not really part of the rules. Going forward, I want one definitive trading session and then I am done for the day so I do not give back profits or feed emotional trading.

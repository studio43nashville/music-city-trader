Why I built this setup

The technical side took me months to figure out.

“The goal is to help you understand and operate your platform so you can focus on learning and testing your own trading plan.”

When I moved from binary options to futures, the technology felt like a whole new world. Connecting my data and brokerage, configuring a trade copier across prop-firm accounts, and learning the platform took months.

As a Mac user, I moved my futures workflow to a Windows VPS — a remote PC that stays on in a data center. I use NinjaTrader with the Replikanto trade copier. It has given me a more reliable workflow for copying trades across supported accounts, but performance still depends on the VPS, internet connection, platform, data provider, configuration, and account rules.

Once my platform was organized, I could focus more of my time on developing and testing my own strategy. The tools help me apply an order-management plan; they do not predict winning trades or guarantee a prop-firm pass.
